# Verifiable financial participation history

Arth exposes **verifiable financial participation history derived from actual contribution, payout, discount, and cycle activity**. It does not produce a traditional credit score, a guarantee of creditworthiness, an AI-generated risk rating, or proof that a member will meet future obligations. No weights or presentation strings are stored on-chain.

`get_financial_history(wallet)` is a public read of aggregate facts in this CommunityPool contract: completed contributions and amount contributed, cycles joined and completed, payout count and total amount, post-payout contributions, discounts earned and claimed, cycle IDs, and current-cycle payout and contribution progress. No wallet authorization is needed to inspect these public protocol facts. `get_cycle_history(cycle_id, wallet)` returns the same wallet's per-cycle contribution obligations and progress, contributions before and after payout, payout amount and round number, discount earned, claimed, and still claimable, and cycle completion. Both reads leave storage and token balances unchanged.

A cycle is joined when the admin includes an active member in `create_cycle`. A cycle is completed for a member only when the cycle's final distinct payout settles **and** that member's completed contributions equal their expected contributions. The completed count increments once at that transition. Historical cycle records remain addressable by cycle ID after a new cycle starts; the aggregate history includes the member's cycle ID list so a frontend can retrieve them. Aggregate counters persist across cycles.

A contribution made before a member's payout increments the cycle's before-payout count. A contribution made in a later round after payout increments both the cycle's after-payout count and the member's aggregate post-payout count. The per-cycle read also reports contributions due after payout to date and the total required after payout through cycle completion. For example, a Round 2 winner in a ten-round cycle has eight later contributions to complete. Payout amounts come from successful `settle` calls; earned discount comes from credits assigned there. Claiming transfers an existing credit and increases *claimed* discount, leaving *earned* discount unchanged.

Portability is wallet-based within the MVP. A frontend or another Arth community can read a wallet's history from this contract by its contract ID, including completed cycles after that wallet joins another cycle or community. Each community is a separate contract; the MVP has no cross-contract registry or automatic combined history. The reader must know the relevant community contract IDs and combine those public reads if desired.

The protocol has no calendar schedule or overdue/default transition. `expected_contributions` and `completed_contributions` show obligations and recorded payments, but an unpaid current round is **not** labeled a missed contribution. Pre-existing missed/default fields are not incremented by this step and are omitted from the financial-history reads. The contract cannot claim a late or missed payment without a defined deadline rule.
